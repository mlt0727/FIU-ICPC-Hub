### Balloon Running — PG6 106, 1:30–6:00 PM · 9 people
**Source: Lingtong** (Document 2: Balloon Squad Guideline), reformatted.

**1. Roles** (decide before the contest starts)
- **Leader**: watches the spreadsheet and the Kattis standings. Tells runners which balloon goes to which team. Makes sure every team gets a balloon for every problem it solves.
- **Balloon runners**: deliver balloons quickly to teams that solve a problem, and tie each balloon securely near the team's table.
- **Balloon squad**: inflates and prepares balloons in advance, so runners always have balloons ready.

**2. Spreadsheet**
- Enter all D1 and D2 team names into the spreadsheet.
- Check that each row matches the right team.

**3. Sort the balloons**
- List all the balloon colors you have.
- Sort them by quantity, most to fewest.

**4. Inflate in advance**
- Start with the color you have the most of.
- Tie each balloon to a string and keep them grouped by color.
- Simple, appropriate drawings are OK. **No words** on balloons.

**5. Matching colors to problems**
- Don't assign colors to problems before the contest starts.
- When a problem is solved for the first time, give it the color with the most balloons left.
- Color that problem's column in the spreadsheet with its balloon color, so everyone knows the pairing.

**6. During the contest**
- When a team solves a problem, the leader calls out the **balloon color** and the **team name**.
- A runner quickly takes that balloon to the team and ties it near their table.
- Keep going as more problems are solved.
- The leader regularly checks that every team has the right balloons for what it has solved.

**7. End of the contest**
- The scoreboard freezes **one hour before the end** (6:00 PM).
- After the freeze, no new balloons are delivered.
- Make sure every delivery from before the freeze is done.

**中文**
**1. 分工**（比赛开始前分好）
- **组长（Leader）**：看着表格和 Kattis 排名网站，告诉跑气球的人哪个气球送给哪个队，确保每个队解出的每道题都拿到了气球。
- **送气球（Balloon runners）**：哪个队解出题目，就尽快把气球送过去，牢牢系在那个队的桌子旁边。
- **准备气球（Balloon squad）**：提前把气球吹好、准备好，保证送气球的人随时有现成的气球。

**2. 表格**
- 把所有 D1 和 D2 的队名填进表格。
- 核对每一行对应的队伍都正确。

**3. 整理气球**
- 列出现有的所有气球颜色。
- 按数量从多到少排序。

**4. 提前吹气球**
- 从数量最多的颜色开始吹。
- 每个气球系上绳子，按颜色放好。
- 可以画简单、得体的图案，但**不要写字**。

**5. 颜色和题目的对应**
- 比赛开始前不要给题目分配颜色。
- 某道题第一次被解出时，把剩下最多的那个颜色分给它。
- 在表格里把这道题那一列涂成对应的气球颜色，大家就都知道了。

**6. 比赛中**
- 有队伍解出题目时，组长喊出**气球颜色**和**队名**。
- 送气球的人马上把气球送到那个队，系在桌子旁边。
- 随着更多题目被解出，一直这样做下去。
- 组长要定期检查：每个队解出的题目都拿到了对应颜色的气球。

**7. 比赛结束**
- 比赛结束前**一小时**（下午 6:00）排名会封榜。
- 封榜以后不再送新气球。
- 确认封榜前的所有气球都已经送到。

> 说明：Balloon Running 的排班是 1:30–6:00 PM，正好在封榜时结束，和第 7 条一致。

There are two lines: one for students, one for coaches.

## Students
1. Ask for the student's name and school. Find the student on the sign-in sheet and have them sign it first.
2. Find their lanyard in the vertical storage unit behind you and put it inside the registration folder.
3. Ask for their T-shirt size. They get a purple shirt and a blue shirt in that size.
4. Remind them that they must wear the **blue shirt** during the actual contest.

## Coaches (brighter blue folder)
1. Ask for the coach's name and school. Find the coach on the sign-in sheet and have them sign it first.
2. Find their lanyard in the vertical storage unit behind you and put it inside the coach's registration folder (the brighter blue one).
3. Explain the menu order form inside the folder: they fill in how many dinner meals of each kind they want for their students. We need it back **before the contest starts**.
4. Tell them breakfast and coffee are waiting for them in **Room 112**.
5. Ask for their T-shirt size. They get a purple shirt and a yellow shirt in that size. If they ask for shirts for coaches who aren't here, give them, as long as we aren't running short.
6. Remind them that their students must wear the **blue shirt** during the actual contest.
